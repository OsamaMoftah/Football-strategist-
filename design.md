# Football strategist — interface system

The approved Werkstatt direction uses Jost 700 for headings, Karla for body/UI, a warm putty canvas, black rules and cobalt actions. Yellow marks the ball; red and yellow also distinguish strategy phases. Keep footage in its original colors and editorial photography monochrome.

The review leads with a full-width annotated video, a precise media-clock scrubber and a simple legend. A ruled strip of key moments sits below it, followed by one plain observation and one coaching question. “Watch this moment” seeks and plays. There is no side rail or telemetry grid in the primary flow. Analysis options and exports/evidence are secondary disclosures. The landing preview is genuine annotated output, with embedded media credit; it respects reduced-motion preferences.

Use square edges, no decorative shadows, restrained typography and structural rules. Do not replace this with rounded metric-card grids, neon glows, invented metrics or decorative AI gradients. Values must come from the active clip or carry a synthetic label.

Canonical tokens and responsive rules live in `src/app/werkstatt.css`. `tokens.css`/`globals.css` retain base and synthetic-sample styles. Canvas colors use the same cobalt/yellow/ink vocabulary. Team swatches are derived from observed pixels rather than a brand palette.

Required checks: 320, 375, 414, 768 and 1050 px, full desktop, keyboard tabs, labels/focus, lost-ball states, worker progress/cancellation and unchanged aspect ratio. The footage must keep its source aspect ratio at every width; moments and explanations stack on narrow screens.
